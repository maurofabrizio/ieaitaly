PROTECTION STARTS HERE

The beach is an ecosystem.
Get to know it to protect it.

Between the beach and the dunes lies a little-known, often overlooked world. With the web app, you can learn about it, respect it and contribute to protecting a rare species.

01 / DISCOVER

Discover the Kentish plover.

Learn about the species, its characteristics and the environments where it lives: beaches, dunes and salt marshes. A small inhabitant of our coasts with great conservation value.

02 / RESPECT

Protect the species.

Read the guidelines and share what you learn with others. Understanding the Kentish plover’s habits helps reduce disturbance in the places where it nests.

03 / TAKE PART

Tell us what you see.

Have you spotted a Kentish plover or one of its nests? Would you like to report litter or potential sources of disturbance? The citizen science section lets you send in your observation and contribute to the knowledge needed to conserve the species.

THE BEACH, AN ENVIRONMENT TO PROTECT

For us, just a beach;
for it, a place to feed,
breed and find shelter.

LIFE ALEXANDRO is the European project dedicated to conserving the Kentish plover and its coastal habitats. The web app is one of its tools, available to everyone who cares about conserving this species: a kind of “Swiss army knife” that gives everyone the opportunity to play their part; a meeting point for knowledge, participation and respect.

The first rule? Observe without disturbing.Keep your distance from animals and nests, respect marked areas and do not approach to take a photograph.

BEFORE YOU GO

Everything at your
fingertips.

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. It is a Progressive Web App: it opens in your smartphone browser. You can add it to your Home screen using your browser options, when available.

Can I submit an observation?

Yes. The web app includes a citizen science section for submitting observations. Only report what you can see while keeping a respectful distance, without approaching animals or nests.

Is there an area for operators?

Yes. The restricted area allows authorised operators to access monitoring tools with their own credentials.
