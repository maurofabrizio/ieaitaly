Partnership

  • Istituto di Ecologia Applicata (Coordinator):Institute of Applied Ecology (lead partner): The Institute of Applied Ecology (IEA) is a nonprofit organization founded in 1987 that aims to provide support to public and private institutions, both in Italy and abroad, for the implementation of initiatives related to the management of natural resources, particularly wildlife and terrestrial and marine habitats.
  • Croatian Society for Birds and Nature Protection: The Croatian Society for Birds and Nature Protection is an organization dedicated to the protection of birds and biodiversity in Croatia. It promotes the conservation of natural habitats, as well as research and monitoring activities. It also carries out environmental education and public awareness initiatives.
  • Ente di Gestione per i Parchi e la Biodiversità-Delta del Po: Since January 1, 2012, the Management Authority for Parks and Biodiversity – Po Delta has managed the protected areas of the Po Delta regional macro-area, including the Regional Park established in 1988, as well as regional nature reserves, some state nature reserves, and other areas subject to protection for the purpose of biodiversity conservation.
  • Istituto Superiore per la Protezione e la Ricerca Ambientale (ISPRA): ISPRA serves citizens and institutions, also supporting the policies of the Ministry of the Environment, Land and Sea Protection. It is responsible for technical and scientific matters related to environmental prevention and protection.
  • Legambiente Nazionale: Legambiente is Italy’s largest environmental organization. It carries out numerous awareness-raising and informational campaigns on environmental issues. It works to fight environmental crime and promotes solidarity and peace. It operates at the local, national, and European levels to promote sustainable development, the green economy, and adaptation to climate change.
  • LIPU ODV: LIPU is an Italian environmental organization dedicated to the protection of birds and biodiversity. Founded in 1965, it promotes the conservation of natural habitats and raises environmental awareness. It manages nature reserves and sanctuaries, conducts scientific research, and combats threats such as poaching and pollution.
  • Ministero della Difesa Arma dei Carabinieri - Comando Unità Forestali, Ambientali e Agroalimentari (CUFA): CUFA is a specialized unit of the Carabinieri Corps under the Ministry of Defense. It is responsible for protecting the environment, forests, biodiversity, and the agri-food sector. It carries out activities related to the monitoring, prevention, and prosecution of environmental crimes and crimes against natural heritage.
  • Parco Nazionale del Circeo: Circeo National Park is a protected natural area established in 1934 along the coast of Lazio. It encompasses diverse habitats such as forests, dunes, coastal lakes, and wetlands, all rich in biodiversity. It plays a vital role in the conservation of animal and plant species and in the protection of the Mediterranean landscape.
  • Parco Regionale della Maremma: The Maremma Regional Park is a protected area along the Tuscan coast established in 1975. It encompasses natural habitats such as pine forests, marshes, hills, and pristine stretches of coastline. It is known for its rich biodiversity and for the conservation of Mediterranean ecosystems.
  • Parco Regionale Migliarino San Rossore Massaciuccoli: The Migliarino San Rossore Massaciuccoli Regional Park is a large nature reserve established in 1979 between Pisa and Lucca.  It includes forests, dunes, wetlands, and Lake Massaciuccoli. It plays a vital role in the conservation of biodiversity and the coastal landscape.
  • Riserve Naturali Regionali Orientate del Litorale Tarantino: The Taranto Coastal Regional Nature Reserves are protected areas along the Ionian coast of Apulia. They safeguard coastal habitats such as dunes, pine forests, and wetlands of great ecological value, contributing to the conservation of animal and plant species.
  • SMART Revolution Srl: Smart Revolution is a consulting company that supports businesses and organizations in EU project planning, from identifying European funding opportunities to comprehensive project management. It specializes in developing international partnerships and participating in EU programs. Smart Revolution has extensive experience in LIFE projects focused on sustainability, the environment, and biodiversity.
  • Università di Pisa – Dipartimento di Biologia: The Department of Biology at the University of Pisa is an academic department dedicated to research and teaching in the biological sciences. It conducts research on biodiversity, ecology, genetics, and biotechnology. It trains students and researchers, contributing to scientific and environmental progress.
