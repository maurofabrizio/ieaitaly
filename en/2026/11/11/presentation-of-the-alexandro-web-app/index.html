Presentation of the alexandro web app

11 November 2026

On November 19, 2026, at 9:30 a.m. at the Granaio Lorenese in Alberese (GR), in the Maremma Regional Park, the Alexandro web app will be presented.

This will be an opportunity to take stock of the project, engage with researchers, and begin familiarizing yourself with this new, important tool, which will allow both beachgoers and researchers (in different ways) to collect data essential for the conservation of the species.

The Alexandro web app—which is free and easy to use—will allow all citizens to contribute to monitoring the Kentish plover and its habitat by reporting sightings of the birds, eggs, plastic debris, and potential predators directly from their cell phones. But that’s not all: through the web app, users will also be able to access in-depth information on species similar to the Kentish plover and aspects of its biology that will help them better understand this fascinating species.

The information collected by citizens will be validated by a group of experts and will help expand our knowledge of the species’ presence and its conservation challenges throughout Italy. Data from the Alexandro web app will be integrated into the National Biodiversity Network (NBB), promoted by the Ministry of the Environment and managed by the National Institute for Environmental Protection and Research (ISPRA).

Schedule for the Day:

9:30 a.m. – Participant registration

10:00 a.m. – Opening remarks by Simone Rusci, President of the Maremma Regional Park.

10:20 a.m. – Marco Lucchesi (IEA, Coordinator of the LIFE Alexandro Project). The LIFE Alexandro Project: Objectives, Tools, and Characteristics.

10:40 a.m. – Simona Imperio (ISPRA). Monitoring the Kentish plover along the Italian coast: the role of the LIFE Alexandro project in the national context.

11:00 a.m. – Cristian Di Stefano (ISPRA). The National Biodiversity Network.

11:20 a.m. – Andrea Sforzi (ATS Alexandro). The Alexandro web app: from basic concept to conceptual development.

11:40 a.m. – Marco Anselmi (ATS Alexandro). Overview of the Alexandro web app and a hands-on demonstration of its use.

12.00 - 12.30 – Questions from the audience

The event is free and open to the public.
