LIFE Alexandro at Punta Faro

On October 7, 2026, a joint IEA-UNIPI-LIPU site visit was conducted at the Punta Faro project site in Lignano Sabbiadoro (UD), to measure the dune bands included in habitat types 2110 (“embryonic dunes”) and 2120 (“shifting dunes”), both of Community interest.

Punta Faro is known as the “Sanderling Beach” due to the numerous nesting sites recorded over the past five years, during which a sandbar gradually formed along the bank of the channel connecting the Marano Lagoon to the sea. This sandy environment, one of the most important nesting sites for the Kentish plover along the entire northern Adriatic coast, has undergone several changes due to currents and storm surges. Our goal, within the project, is to promote the stabilization of the beach through environmental improvement measures and the planting of native species (such as Ammophila arenaria and Cakile maritima), while simultaneously removing the non-native ones such as Amorpha fruticosa.
