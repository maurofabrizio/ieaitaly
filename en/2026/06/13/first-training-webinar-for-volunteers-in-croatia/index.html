First training webinar for volunteers in croatia

13 June 2026

On June 9, 2026, the first training course was held remotely for volunteers who will be involved in monitoring and surveying Kentish plover nests in Croatia.

10 volunteers participated in the webinar organized by LIPU, ISPRA, IEA and CSBNP. During the webinar, monitoring techniques and measures to be implemented for nest protection were presented.
