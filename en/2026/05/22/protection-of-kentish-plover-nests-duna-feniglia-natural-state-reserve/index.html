Protection of Kentish plover nests-Duna Feniglia Natural State Reserve

In the framework of the project, measures to protect Kentish plover nests have begun in all project areas across Italy. In the Duna Feniglia Natural State Reserve, five nests were identified, and steps were immediately taken to protect them by installing fencing and informational signs. Under the scientific supervision of the Tuscan Ornithological Center (COT), which was commissioned by IEA to monitor the species, CUFA staff and volunteers from COT and from the OA WWF Grosseto—who participated in the first training course—regularly monitor the area to collect data and provide accurate information to the public.
